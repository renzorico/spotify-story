      Every arc leaves Spain for a country where I pressed play, and thicker means more hours there. Hover a country, or use the arrow keys, for its hours and what I played most there.

        How it was made, in six steps
        1. RequestExtended streaming history from Spotify.
        2. LoadJSON into DuckDB, music only, 2021 to 2026.
        3. CleanUTC to Madrid time; IP addresses dropped.
        4. DefineA skip is a play under 30 seconds.
        5. AggregateBy month, week, hour, artist and country.
        6. RenderD3 in the browser, no network calls.
