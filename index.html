        How it was made.

        Spotify sends a full listening log on request; a Python script turns it into the aggregates on this page and nothing else. Every chart is drawn in the browser from that one small file, with no network calls.

        1. RequestExtended streaming history from Spotify's privacy page.
        2. LoadJSON logs into DuckDB, music only, 2021 to 2026.
        3. CleanUTC to Madrid time; IP addresses dropped.
        4. DefineA skip is a play under 30 seconds.
        5. AggregateBy month, week, hour, artist and country.
        6. RenderD3 in the browser, from one 84 KB file.
