    How is this made?

      The year as a clock, January at the top. Each dot is a song I played in at least two different years, placed at the time of year it peaks; the further out, the more its plays crowd into that one season. Hover a dot for the song.

        Every arc leaves Spain for a country where I pressed play; brighter and thicker means more hours. Click a country for its top artists and songs. Drag to pan, and zoom with the buttons, a pinch or Ctrl and the scroll wheel.

          I'm Renzo, a data scientist based in Barcelona. Everything above comes from my own Spotify export, processed on my laptop and turned into one finding per screen.

          How it was made, in six steps
          1. RequestExtended streaming history from Spotify.
          2. LoadJSON into DuckDB, music only, 2021 to 2026.
          3. CleanUTC to Madrid time; IP addresses dropped.
          4. TagGenres by hand for my top 150 artists.
          5. AggregateBy month, day, artist, genre and country.
          6. RenderD3 in the browser, no network calls.
